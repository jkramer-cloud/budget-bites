# Budget Bites v5 — 16-bit Kitchen Visual Upgrade

## New
- Walkable 16-bit kitchen using the scene from the approved concept art.
- Transparent chef character based on the concept, animated while walking.
- Six detailed close-up scenes: pantry, sink, prep, mixing, stove, plating.
- Close-ups open after moving to the correct station; tasks can be completed through the illustrated scene or control panel.
- Animated water, bubbles, ingredient highlights, chopping effects, bowl stirring, pouring, stove steam/flame, and serving sparkles.
- Recipe quest panels, active station highlight, live household budget and points.
- Keyboard, on-screen directional buttons, click-to-pathfind, station shortcut travel.
- Mobile responsive station scenes and reduced-motion accessibility support.

## Preserved
- Meal planning, household settings, allergy checks, grocery shopping, receipts, inventory, nutrition reports, save/reset and exported backups.
- 12-step hands-on Banana Cinnamon Oats lesson with safety checks.
- Existing guided cooking options for other recipes.
- Existing kitchen recipe JSON schema for future new recipes.

## Known limitations
- Other recipes have not yet received 12-step graphical cooking lessons.
- Station art comes from single rendered panels; motion currently uses overlay animations rather than separate hand-drawn animation frames for every food item.
